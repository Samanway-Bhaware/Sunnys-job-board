# Your job board — how to use it

This is a private page that looks for communications, marketing and business development
jobs at creative and cultural organisations, and keeps track of the ones you're interested
in. Only you can see it.

## Opening it

The first time you open the link on a phone or computer, it asks for a password. Type it once
and that device remembers you. You'll only be asked again if you sign out or the password is
changed.

When you open the page it checks for new roles if it hasn't looked recently. It takes about a
minute — you'll see a little chip for each role type while it searches. Anything found since
your last visit gets a **New** label. The **Refresh** button at the top searches again straight
away.

## The list

Every row is a job. **View listing** opens the original advert in a new tab — always read that
before applying, because the search is done by an AI and can be out of date or wrong about
details.

Each row shows where the job is (Remote, Hybrid or On-site), the hours, the salary if it was
given, and the closing date. Roles closing within a week are marked in red.

**Sponsorship** comes from what the advert says: *Offered*, *Not offered*, or *Not stated*.
A separate **Licensed sponsor** label means the organisation is on the UK government's list of
employers allowed to sponsor visas. That doesn't guarantee this particular job is sponsored —
it just means it's possible to ask.

## Narrowing the list

- **Tabs** along the top switch between role types. **All roles** shows everything.
- **Search** box: type any part of an organisation, job title or place.
- **Remote / Hybrid / On-site** and **Full-time / Part-time**: click to switch on, click again
  to switch off. You can pick more than one.
- **Include roles where hours aren't stated**: leave this on unless you only want adverts
  that clearly say full- or part-time.
- **Location**: tick places, or type part of a place name.
- **Visa sponsorship**: *Offered in listing*, *Offered or employer is a licensed sponsor*, or
  *Hide roles that rule out sponsorship*.
- **Hide closed** is on by default.
- **Show**: *All roles*, *Roles I'm tracking*, or *Not for me*.
- **Sort**: closing soonest, newest first, or salary high to low.

The page remembers your filters. **Clear filters** puts everything back.

## Keeping track

Use **My status** on any row: Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected, or **Not for me**
(which hides it — find it again under Show → Not for me). The day you first set *Applied* is
recorded for you.

**Add a note** under the status box keeps a short note with the job.

Jobs you've given a status are never removed, even after they close. Everything else drops
off once it's closed or hasn't been seen for three weeks.

## Adding a role type

Press **+ Add role** at the end of the tabs (or use Settings). Give it a name — say,
"Fundraising" — and, if you like, a few related words. It gets its own tab and the search
runs for it right away. You can have up to ten.

In **Settings** you can rename role types, change their keywords, switch one off without
deleting it, reorder them, or delete one. Deleting keeps any jobs you were tracking under an
**Archived** tab.

## Settings

- **Search**: the sector, the places you want, and a note for the search (for example, that
  you prefer two or three days a week). Change these and the next search uses them.
- **AI model**: which service does the searching. Claude is the default. **Test** checks it works.
- **API keys**: optional. If you add your own key, the site uses it instead of the shared one
  and the daily search limit no longer applies. Paste the key and press Save — it's stored
  encrypted and never shown again. **Remove my key** goes back to the shared one.
- **Your tracked roles**: downloads a spreadsheet of every job you've given a status, with
  your notes.
- **Sign out of this device** if you're on a shared computer.

## If something looks wrong

- *"The search didn't finish"* — press Refresh. Your saved roles are still there.
- *"You've used all … searches for today"* — the shared key has a daily limit; it resets
  tomorrow, or add your own key in Settings.
- *"UK sponsor register unavailable"* — the government file couldn't be downloaded just now.
  Everything else works; the *licensed sponsor* option comes back when it does.
